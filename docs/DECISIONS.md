| D-030 | 2026-10-31 | **Rules 0.7.0: hunger costs 30 cohesion a day at a full food shortfall, not 100** (G-017). Straggling and foraging on the march are unchanged. The value comes from the historical record before any batch was run: the central army lost a third of its men to sickness and straggling in the 52 days to Smolensk and still fought, and modern data show underfeeding saps strength over weeks. What-if runs over the plausible range (20–60) showed the effect before adoption. The balance batch gains rule overrides for such runs | Collapse from hunger in every advancing game was not supported by the history (HISTORICAL_RESEARCH.md, hunger; GAMEPLAY_BALANCING.md) | User approval ("Adopt 30 a day") |
| D-031 | 2026-10-31 | **Rules 0.8.0: contacts corrected** after the independent review of commit 1ade716. Forces move hour by hour, all together. Contact happens at places, never on the road. A force may not set out along a road with an oncoming enemy column, and a seeded draw decides who gets onto a road when both ends would set out in the same hour. No overtaking. A force where the enemy stands does not march on. Reinforcements of either side join a battle at the contacts boundary and fight from the next daylight sub-step. A force falling back leaves at once, onto the road, and marches the rest later. A halt clears every French standing route. Also: fodder-only shortfalls are logged, saved comparisons keep the fog of war, "Save run" became "Save for comparison", and the batch fails on invariant violations. | D-032 | 2026-10-31 | **Rules 0.9.0: horses, carrying and foraging reach** (G-019). Every wagon needs draught horses, in the wagon trains and in each group's baggage; only wagons with their horses carry a load. Wagon-train horses graze, then eat the fodder they carry (keeping back what they need to get home), then their depot's. Draught horses eat and starve with a group's other horses. A group marches only with what its men (four days' rations) and horse-drawn wagons can carry: setting out, it leaves the rest behind, fodder first, back into its depot or abandoned, and the staff says so before you commit. Depot issue stops at what can be carried. Dispersed foraging reaches a neighbouring district only within 22 km of it. The Russian armies' placeholder starting fodder is trimmed to what they can carry. March speed still follows the infantry | Stage B of the independent review.
