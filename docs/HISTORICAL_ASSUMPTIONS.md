# Historical and Game Assumptions Register

Every figure, date or rule the game relies on is listed here with its status (HISTORICAL_RESEARCH.md): **Verified**, **Disputed**, **Unverified**, or **Game assumption** (a design choice, openly labelled).

## Game assumptions

| G-004 | Outcome thresholds (provisional; revised in rules 0.10.0, D-035): the Russian main armies are the forces holding at least a quarter of the Russian strength at the start, fixed for the game (D-037); decisive defeat when they lose ≥ 30% of their starting strength within 4 days in battles the French won and in which the Russians broke, routed in the field or trapped with no road out (an orderly withdrawal never counts, H-018); cut off when at least 30,000 French hold Valutino or Dorogobuzh, east of the Russian main armies, for 2 consecutive sub-steps; escaped intact below 10% lost; capable means ≥ 75% of men and cohesion ≥ 500; sustainable means every group has ≥ 2 days' food counting what it carries, what its wagon train can bring within those 2 days with the horses it has, its depot, and its share of the forage within reach (each district's food shared among the groups that reach it, D-037); collapse below cohesion 250 or at ≥ 50% of men lost | set in testing | §2.13 | `rules.outcome`; the line-delivery test (*T₁*) is not used yet |

| G-016 | Captured stores and forward depots: a depot changes hands when only the enemy holds it, with 50–90% of its stores burned first (drawn from the seed per capture). The staff founds at most one depot a turn, where French troops stand 40–80 km beyond the nearest depot. Line trains haul at most 80 km (about 4½ days at 18 km/day) and finish a loaded trip before taking a new one. The scenario has two line trains: 300 wagons at Vitebsk and 250 at Orsha | Game assumptions | the burned share reflects the Russian practice of destroying magazines before abandoning them (H-006 for Smolensk, to source); spacing and haul make stores move up in relays (§2.6). | G-017 | Hunger and foraging, calibrated on the history (**adopted in rules 0.7.0**, D-030): cohesion loss at a full food shortfall 30 a day (plausible 20–60; was 100); straggling 2% of men a day at a full shortfall (plausible 1–3%; unchanged); foraging on the march 0.2 rations a man a day (plausible 0.1–0.5; unchanged); recovery +10 a day when fed (unchanged). Returning stragglers are not modelled | Game assumptions inside ranges derived from H-014 to H-017, L-007 and L-008 | the present −100 a day would break the army in 2½–3½ weeks at the historical loss rate; the real army marched 7½ weeks and still fought (HISTORICAL_RESEARCH.md, hunger) | `rules.attrition`, `rules.forage` |
| G-019 | Horses, carrying and foraging reach (rules 0.9.0, **adopted**, D-032): 2 draught horses per 800 kg wagon (plausible 1–4), for wagon trains and for each group's own wagons, on top of its saddle and artillery horses; a man carries 3 kg of stores, four days' rations (plausible 3–6 days); a group carries what its men and its horse-drawn wagons can take; setting out on the march, it leaves the rest behind (fodder first, then food, ammunition last), back into the depot if it stands at one, otherwise abandoned; at a depot it draws only what it can carry; wagon-train horses graze along the way at the marching rate, then eat the fodder they carry, then their depot's; a dispersed group forages a neighbouring district only if that district begins within 11 km, i.e. its place lies within 22 km by road: half the width of the source's foraging square for extended operations (plausible 7.5–15 km). *Corrected in rules 0.12.0 (D-037):* it was 22 km, from reading L-011's square widths as radii March speed still follows the infantry | Game assumptions inside ranges from L-009 to L-011 | connect horses to transport, make carrying finite, and bound foraging by distance (independent review, stage B); the reach corrected after the second review | `rules.draught`, `rules.carrying`, `rules.forage.reachMetres` |
| G-018 | Contact timing (rules 0.8.0, D-031): movement in six hourly shares per sub-step; who gets onto a road first, when enemies at both ends would set out in the same hour, is an even seeded draw; a reinforcement deploys for one sub-step before it fights, like a new battle; a force falling back leaves as a body (concentrated), onto the road, at its own pace (a resting force at a steady pace) | Game assumptions | keep contact at places, make arrival order matter without retroactive combat, and give retreats real time on the road (§3.4a) | `HOURS_PER_SLOT`, `joinReinforcements`, `fallBack` |
| G-015 | Detection: 15 km base, plus 4 km per 1,000 horses, capped at 60 km; strength estimates ±20%, rounded to the nearest hundred; updated at the end of each turn | Game assumptions | gives cavalry its scouting role (§2.8) | `rules.intel` |
