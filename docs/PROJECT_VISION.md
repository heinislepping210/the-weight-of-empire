# Project Vision

## The game

Players command Napoleon's Grande Armée in the summer of 1812, between Vitebsk and Smolensk. They try to reach an extraordinary objective despite distance, supply, horses, weather and an opponent that does not want to fight on their terms. The question at the heart of the game: *how do you pursue an extraordinary ambition without destroying the resources and people needed to achieve it?*

It is entertainment first: a serious, historically grounded strategy game, not a leadership test or a training product. The Command Challenge is a short, shareable cut of the same engine.

## Priorities (in order)

1. Meaningful gameplay
2. A convincing, responsive campaign map
3. Historical credibility
4. Replayability and fair decisions
5. Usability
6. Technical reliability
7. Visual design
8. Cinematic polish
9. Additional features

## 10 Scope boundaries (unchanged from your brief, plus mine)

Not in scope without your explicit approval:
- 3D;
- multiplayer;
- accounts or servers;
- paid or live AI;
- open world;
- multiple commanders;
- diplomacy;
- leaderboards;
- social features;
- subscriptions;
- cinematics that can't be skipped.

**Additions:**
- playing the Russian side (the symmetric rules make it possible later);
- extra difficulty levels;
- localisation (text is kept in one place to make it possible later).

**Excellence before expansion:** The Road to Smolensk starts only after The Vitebsk Decision passes M4 and you approve it.
