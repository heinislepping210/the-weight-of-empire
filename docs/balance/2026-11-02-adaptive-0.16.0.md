# Adaptive policy on held-out seeds: rules 0.16.0, seeds 1–1000

Candidates: concentrated-push, southern-envelopment, bait-and-converge, methodical, prudent-halt, rush, concentrated-push-staged, southern-envelopment-hold, concentrated-strike, supplied-halt. The adaptive policy picks one at the first decision from two things the French see: their own strength and the Russians their scouts report (each in thirds), learned on seeds 1–500 and judged on seeds 501–1000. Each scenario is played with 3 sets of dice and scored by its mean rank over them. A lower rank is better; "won" means Decisive Success, Costly Victory or Operational Success, over every game.

**Reading:** a perfect reader of the scenario could improve on the best fixed approach by 0.26 ranks on average (knowing the dice as well, by 0.42); this adaptive policy, reading two figures at the first decision, captures 0.01. That bounds what this policy can read, not what a player can.
